# Persuasion & Shareability Frameworks

## 1. StoryBrand SB7 (Donald Miller)

Premise: "If you confuse, you lose." The **customer is the hero; the brand is the guide.**

The 7-part sequence (apply to any page, bio, video script, pitch):
1. **A Character** — the customer, wanting ONE clearly stated thing.
2. **Has a Problem** — name a villain; articulate the problem at 3 levels: *external* (practical), *internal* (frustration/self-doubt — what people actually buy solutions to), *philosophical* (why it's just plain wrong).
3. **Meets a Guide** — the brand shows **empathy** ("we understand") + **authority** (testimonials, stats, logos — brief).
4. **Who Gives Them a Plan** — a 3–4 step process plan that removes risk/confusion.
5. **And Calls Them to Action** — ONE direct CTA repeated everywhere + one transitional CTA (lead magnet) for the not-yet-ready.
6. **That Helps Them Avoid Failure** — state the stakes (a pinch, not a bludgeon).
7. **And Ends in Success** — the specific after-state; the identity the customer becomes.

**Grunt test:** within 5 seconds a stranger can say what you offer, how it improves life, and how to buy.

**Prompt-ready template:**
> "{audience} wants {desire}. But {villain/problem} means {internal frustration}. {brand} understands ({empathy line}) and has {proof}. The plan: 1) {step} 2) {step} 3) {step}. {cta}. Skip this and {stakes}; do it and {after-state}."

Limits: produces homogenized messaging if applied verbatim; synthesis of narrative theory, not outcome data.

## 2. Jonah Berger — STEPPS (Contagious)

Research base: ~7,000 NYT most-emailed articles, WOM panels, lab experiments. WOM drives an estimated 20–50% of purchases; only ~7% of word of mouth happens online.

1. **Social Currency** — people share what makes them look good: inner remarkability, game mechanics, insider access.
2. **Triggers** — "top of mind, tip of tongue": link the brand to a **frequent environmental cue** (Kit Kat + coffee break). Trigger frequency beats cleverness for sustained WOM. → Choose a {cep}/cue and pair the brand with it relentlessly.
3. **Emotion** — high-arousal emotions drive sharing: awe +30%, anger +34%, anxiety +21% likelihood of viral sharing; **sadness −16%**. → Amplify awe/amusement/anger/anxiety; avoid contentment/sadness framings for shareable content.
4. **Public** — make consumption observable; design behavioral residue (merch, badges, visible rituals).
5. **Practical Value** — "news you can use." **Rule of 100:** under $100 express discounts in %, over $100 in absolute $.
6. **Stories** — information travels inside narratives; ensure **valuable virality**: the brand must be integral to the story, not decoration.

**Shareability checklist (score each 0–2 before publishing):** Does sharing this make the sharer look smart/good? Is there a recurring trigger? Is the dominant emotion high-arousal? Is the behavior/result visible? Is there immediately usable value? Is the brand load-bearing in the story?

## 3. Robert Cialdini — 7 Principles of Influence

1. **Reciprocity** — give first, personalized ("for you") giving amplifies (mint experiments: +3% → +14% → +23% tips). Content form: genuinely free value before any ask.
2. **Commitment & Consistency** — small public commitments escalate. Content form: micro-asks (save, quiz, comment keyword) before conversion asks.
3. **Social Proof** — strongest under uncertainty and from *similar* others ("most guests in this room" +33% vs +26% generic). Content form: specific, similar-peer testimonials; NEVER advertise the prevalence of the undesired behavior.
4. **Liking** — similarity, compliments, cooperation, familiarity. Content form: founder relatability, shared-identity signals.
5. **Authority** — credentials cited once, visibly; most credible when **conceding a weakness first**, then the strongest point.
6. **Scarcity** — loss framing beats gain framing; only genuine limits. Also: always give a "because" (Xerox study: any stated reason lifted compliance 60%→93%).
7. **Unity** — shared identity ("one of us") outperforms liking. Content form: community language, insider rituals, co-creation.

Ethics gate: use a principle only when the underlying claim is true — detection destroys trust (see 02-trust).

## 4. Composite message architecture

For any conversion-oriented asset, layer: SB7 for structure → STEPPS for shareability → Cialdini for persuasion accents → one CTA (see 25-cta-library). For pure brand-building assets, drop the CTA and maximize STEPPS emotion + distinctive assets in the first 2 seconds (see 01-canon attention rules).

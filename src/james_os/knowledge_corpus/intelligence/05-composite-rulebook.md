# Composite Rulebook

## The 10 strategy rules

1. **Allocation:** ~60/40 brand-building : activation, flexed by category (subscription up to 74:26, B2B ~46:54). Sustain share of voice / share of search above market share; +10 ESOV points ≈ +0.5 share points/year.
2. **Audience:** always broad — light and non-buyers drive growth. Loyalty-targeted content is 3–10× less effective for growth. Never require insider knowledge.
3. **Memory:** every asset carries 3–5 distinctive brand assets within the first 1.5–2.5 seconds, mapped to 6–10 category entry points ("when {cep}, think {brand}").
4. **Creative:** emotional > rational for long-term effects (43% vs 13% very-large-profit rates); creative quality ≈ half of sales impact — iterate creative before optimizing targeting.
5. **Equity check:** score content Meaningful + Different + Salient. Difference defends pricing power (+14–37% willingness to pay). Salience alone is a multiplier on nothing.
6. **Trust:** founder/employee voices over corporate voice (78% employer trust; employees > influencers > CEOs). Demonstrate competence and ethics together. Named authors on thought leadership.
7. **Operations:** respond within 24h (73% defect on silence); reply-first culture (replies lift engagement on every platform); original franchises over trends; trend window is 24–48h — join fast or skip.
8. **Channels:** search + short-video social + YouTube spine; social-first for under-35 discovery; optimize for social search and AI assistants; one hero asset repurposed per-platform.
9. **Shareability:** engineer STEPPS (high-arousal emotion, frequent triggers, practical value, brand-integral stories); message with SB7 clarity; persuade with Cialdini truthfully.
10. **Measurement:** judge brand work over ≥12 months; plan on attentive seconds not impressions (8–9s to move intent); track share of search, pricing power, mental availability — not engagement rate alone.

## The universal content rules (hold on every platform, 2026)

1. **Conversation is the ranking currency.** Replying to comments lifts engagement everywhere [measured: +42% Threads, +30% LinkedIn, +21% Instagram, +9% Facebook, +8% X]. X weights a reply-with-author-response ~150× a like. Protect the first hour after posting for replies.
2. **External links are penalized everywhere except Pinterest.** Post native; link in comments/replies.
3. **Dwell formats win:** documents/carousels, threads, albums — anything that extends time-on-post.
4. **Hashtags are dead or dying** on LinkedIn, X, Facebook; marginal on Instagram (1–5 precise). Keyword SEO in captions, voiceover, on-screen text replaced them. TikTok, Pinterest and YouTube are search engines — title to the query.
5. **Vertical (4:5 / 2:3 / 9:16) is the default canvas.**
6. **Retention + shares/sends outrank likes** in every algorithm. Longer short-form (60–180s) is rewarded if completion holds.
7. **Hook before value:** the first line / first 1.5–3 seconds decides distribution. Question hooks are the weakest measured category; story, contrarian and result-first hooks are the strongest.
8. **One CTA per asset.** Comment-keyword CTAs convert best (5–15%); link-in-bio worst (1–3%).
9. **Specificity beats adjectives.** Numbers, names, timeframes.
10. **Consistency beats volume:** posting 20+ weeks out of 26 ≈ 5× the per-post engagement of sporadic posting. Never dark for 2+ weeks.

## Decision heuristics

- New account? → Run playbooks/13-cold-start + templates/24 30-day launch calendar.
- Choosing format? → reach goal: short vertical video; engagement/saves: carousel/document; authority: long-form + named-author thought leadership.
- Trend opportunity? → within 48h AND on-voice AND brand is load-bearing? Join. Else skip (33% embarrassment penalty).
- AI-generated draft? → add human markers (named author, face, opinion, specifics, imperfection) or don't publish; ~1/3 of consumers penalize detectable AI content.
- Budget question? → creative iteration > targeting refinement (49% vs ~11% of sales impact).
- Plateau at ~100K in a niche? → broaden to adjacent topics (documented pattern).

# Consumer & Platform Intelligence

## 1. Sprout Social Index — consumer expectation rules [measured, survey]

- **93%** say brands should keep up with online culture, BUT **33%** find brands jumping on viral trends "embarrassing," and **27%** say a trend's window is only **24–48 hours**. → Rule: culture *fluency* without forced participation; join a trend only if within 24–48h AND on-voice; otherwise skip.
- ~**50%** say **original content** is what makes favorite brands stand out (second only to product quality). → Weight original franchises over trends.
- **73%** will buy from a competitor if a brand doesn't respond on social; brands' actual response rate is only ~7%. → Respond within 24h; treat non-response as measurable churn risk.
- **81%** say social drives impulse purchases; 54% of Gen Z and 47% of Millennials purchase via TikTok.
- Brands are deliberately posting less (9.5 posts/day average, down YoY) while inbound engagement rises (+20% YoY). → Fewer posts + more replies outperforms feed-flooding.

## 2. We Are Social / Meltwater Digital 2026 — usage data [measured, panel]

- 6.04B internet users; **5.66B social identities** (68.7% of population); average user active on **~7 platforms/month**; adults spend **18h36m/week** on social/video feeds (16–24s: 25h45m on video feeds).
- Brand discovery: **search engines 32.6%** (#1), TV ads #2, social ads #3 — but for **ages 16–34, social is the #1 discovery channel**.
- Top three channels combined reach only **62.2%** of online adults — no single-channel strategy achieves full reach.
- **1B+ monthly generative-AI users** — AI-assistant discoverability is now a channel.
- Streaming = 50.4% of global TV time; YouTube alone = 13.1% of US TV viewing.

### Channel-selection directives
1. Default portfolio: search presence + short-video social + YouTube + one community/messaging surface.
2. Under-35 target → lead with social for awareness; optimize for in-platform search (keywords in captions, on-screen text, spoken audio).
3. Repurpose one hero asset across surfaces (audiences overlap across ~7 platforms) but adapt format per platform.
4. Static-only strategies concede the majority of attention minutes — video is mandatory.

## 3. What top performers do differently (Hootsuite / HubSpot / CMI 2026) [measured, survey; vendor caveat]

- **86.4%** of teams use AI; but AI speeds output more than it improves outcomes (95% of B2B marketers use it; only 39% report performance gains). → Use AI for speed; gate publication on human-signal markers (named authors, faces, opinions, imperfection).
- AI-generated content surpassed human content online in 2025; ~1/3 of consumers are **less** likely to choose a brand using obviously AI-made ads; audiences prefer imperfect, naturally paced content.
- **Short-form video = top ROI format (48.6% of marketers)**; Instagram overtook Facebook as the #1 brand platform; TikTok beats X on usage and ROI.
- Top performers: long-term creator partnerships (not one-offs), in-house creators, formal employee-advocacy programs, streamlined approvals (speed-to-trend within the 24–48h window), audience-fit over follower count, business metrics over engagement metrics.
- Influencer marketing used by only 21.2% of brands (whitespace); micro-influencers deliver the strongest ROI.
- B2B winners: documented strategy (97% have one), content relevance/quality (65% cite as top effectiveness driver), sales alignment (45%).

## 4. Operational synthesis

1. Build a rapid-response lane: pre-approved guardrails + fast sign-off so cultural moments can be joined inside 24–48h — or skipped deliberately.
2. Response operations are a growth function, not support: first-hour replies, 24h SLA on all inbound.
3. Original recurring franchises (formats the brand owns) are the highest-return content investment.
4. Few, long-term micro-creator partnerships > one-off big names.
5. Measure business impact (leads, share of search, pricing power), not engagement rate.

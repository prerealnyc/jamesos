# Hook Library

## Performance data [measured where noted]

- **LinkedIn (AuthoredUp, ~310K posts, median ER by hook type):** Story 2.60% > Contrarian 2.31% > Statement 2.27% > Results 2.19% > Question 2.16%. Hooks of 0–40 characters: 2.61% vs 2.08% for 200+ chars — **short hooks win**.
- **Question hooks are the weakest measured category on every platform.** Use for engagement-bait only.
- **Short-form video:** viewers decide in ~2–3 seconds; deliver the hook by 2–2.5s; target ≥60% retention at 3s.
- Rule: hook must fit above the fold (Instagram ~125 chars, LinkedIn ~140–210 chars, X first line).

## A. Curiosity gap

| Template | Platforms |
|---|---|
| "I stopped {common_activity} {timeframe} ago. Here's what happened to {metric}." | LinkedIn, TikTok |
| "The reason {phenomenon} happens is not what you think." | Shorts/TikTok, X |
| "There's a {tool/feature} that {N}% of {audience} ignore. It generated {result} for me." | LinkedIn |

## Generation rules

1. Match hook category to goal: reach → result-first/contrarian; saves → listicle/warning; comments → contrarian/question-bait; authority → story/statistic.
2. Keep under 40 characters where possible (measured 2.61% vs 2.08%).
3. Never resolve the hook before the payoff section (open-loop rule).
4. On video: hook verbally AND in on-screen text simultaneously; brand asset visible within 1.5s.
5. Specificity check: at least one number, name or timeframe in the hook.

# CTA Library

## The iron rule
**One CTA per asset.** Multiple CTAs split action and depress all of them.

## Conversion hierarchy (Instagram-measured; directionally cross-platform)

| Rank | CTA type | Conversion | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Comment-keyword → DM automation** | **5–15%** | Top performer; 2–5× bio links; also farms comment volume (algorithm signal) |
| 2 | **Share CTA** | — | #1 algorithm signal ("send this to a friend who {pain_point}") |
| 3 | **Save CTA** | — | Prioritized by algorithm; best on educational/reference content |
| 4 | **Follow CTA** | low | Works only with an open loop ("follow for part 2"); generic "follow for more" is dead weight |
| 5 | **Link-in-bio** | **1–3%** | Five friction steps; use only when the destination is the point |

## Soft vs hard selection

- **Soft** (invitation, low pressure): "Learn more," "Save this for later," "See how it works." Use: cold audiences, informational content, early funnel, brand-building assets.
- **Hard** (direct, urgency/exclusivity): "Buy now," "Start your free trial," "Book the call today." Use: warm audiences, high-intent surfaces, launches, genuine deadlines only.
- Cold → saves/shares. Warm → keyword/DM. Hot → booking/purchase.
- Thread pairing: soft CTA in the penultimate unit + hard CTA in the final unit.
- Mid-video internal CTA: one specific comment question at a natural pause — never "like and subscribe" walls.

## Prompt-ready templates

**Comment-keyword:**
- "Comment {KEYWORD} and I'll DM you {asset}."
- "Comment {KEYWORD} and I'll send you the exact {template/system}."
- "Want the {asset}? Comment {KEYWORD} below."
Rule: single memorable word people won't type accidentally; deliver instantly via automation.

**Share:** "Send this to a {person} who's struggling with {pain_point}." · "Share this to your story if you agree with #{N}."

**Save:** "Save this for your next {activity}." · "Save this — you'll need it when {situation}."

**Follow (open-loop only):** "Follow for part 2 — {tease}." · "I post {content_type} for {audience} every {cadence} — follow so you don't miss {next_topic}."

**Engagement/reply:** "Which one would you add?" · "What am I missing?" · "In one word: {topic}?" · "Comment '1' if you've experienced this."

**Conversion (warm):** "DM me '{KEYWORD}' and I'll send the details." · "Link's in the first comment." · "{N} spots — book at {place}."

## CTA-by-goal matrix

| Content goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Reach/distribution | Share |
| Authority/education | Save |
| List building | Comment-keyword → DM → email |
| Series retention | Follow + open loop |
| Comments/velocity | Question or "comment {word}" bait |
| Revenue (warm) | DM keyword or booking link in comment |
| Brand-building asset | Often NO CTA — let it breathe (60/40 rule) |

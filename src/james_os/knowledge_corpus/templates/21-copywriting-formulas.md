# Copywriting Formulas

## AIDA — Attention · Interest · Desire · Action
Classic 4-stage ladder.
**Template:** "{hook}. {fresh_info_why_it_matters}. {what_life_looks_like_with_it}. {cta}."
**Example:** "Your reels get 200 views. The algorithm buries videos with weak first frames. Imagine 10× reach from the same effort. Save this checklist."

## PAS — Problem · Agitate · Solution
Pain-first; the most-used social formula.
**Template:** "{audience} struggle with {pain_point}. But {it's_worse_than_you_think_because}. Smart {audience} {solution/method}."
**Variants:** PADC (adds Discredit other solutions before yours); CSI (Challenge → Solution → Invitation).

## BAB — Before · After · Bridge
Show the transformation, then the vehicle.
**Template:** "You're {current_painful_state}. Imagine {desired_state}. Here's the bridge: {method} — {first_step}."
**Example:** "You post daily and get crickets. Imagine 3 inbound leads a week. The bridge: a 4-part hook system — here it is."

## PPPP — Picture · Promise · Prove · Push
**Template:** "{vivid_scene_of_the_dream}. {how_this_transforms_it}. {proof: testimonials/data/demo}. {urgent_nudge_cta}."
**Ray Edwards variant:** Problem → Promise → Proof → Proposal.

## 4U Headline Formula (AWAI/Bly)
Score every headline 1–4 on each U; rewrite until ≥3 score high: **Useful · Urgent · Unique · Ultra-specific.**
**Template:** "{ultra_specific_benefit} + {timeframe/urgency} + {unique_mechanism}."
**Example:** "Free up 2 hours a day with the 3-touch inbox method (works this week)."

## FAB — Feature · Advantage · Benefit
Translate specs into outcomes.
**Template:** "{feature} (what it is) {advantage} (why it beats alternatives) — {benefit} (what it personally means for {audience})."
**Example:** "Batch scheduler (feature) posts to 6 platforms at once (advantage) — you get Sundays back (benefit)."

## Slippery Slide (Joe Sugarman)
Every element exists only to get the next line read; no exit ramps until the CTA.
**Rules:** first line ≤8 words and impossible not to continue; escalate curiosity each line; short sentences; the sole purpose of sentence 1 is to get sentence 2 read.
**Example first line:** "I almost quit last March."

## Selection heuristic

| Goal | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cold audience, aware of pain | PAS |
| Transformation offer | BAB |
| Feature-rich product | FAB |
| Long-form sales asset | PPPP or AIDA |
| Any headline/title | run 4U scoring |
| Any long caption/thread | apply slippery slide rules on top |

**Universal rules:** one idea per sentence; specificity beats adjectives; write at 8th-grade level; the formula structures the middle — the hook library (20) supplies the opening and the CTA library (25) supplies the close.

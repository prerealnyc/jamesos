# Brand Intelligence Corpus

**Purpose:** Training/reference corpus for an AI brand-management platform. Contains the general intelligence and prompt-ready templates needed to take any brand — in any niche — from zero followers to high visibility, engagement and social authority.

**Compiled:** July 2026, from primary research: IPA/Binet & Field, Ehrenberg-Bass, Kantar BrandZ, Edelman Trust Barometer, Nielsen/NCSolutions, attention research (Amplified Intelligence, Lumen), Sprout Social Index, We Are Social/Meltwater Digital 2026, Hootsuite/HubSpot/CMI 2026 reports, large-scale benchmark studies (Buffer 2M+ posts, Socialinsider, Rival IQ, AuthoredUp 310K posts), and documented case studies.

## Structure

```
intelligence/     WHY things work — marketing science, trust research, consumer data
playbooks/        WHAT to do — stage-gated growth, loops, case studies, cold starts
templates/        HOW to write it — prompt-ready hooks, scripts, post templates, CTAs
platform-specs/   WHERE it runs — per-platform formats, specs, algorithms, timing
```

## Variable dictionary

All templates share one variable vocabulary:

`{audience}` who the content serves · `{niche}` topic domain · `{pain_point}` their problem · `{result}` quantified outcome · `{timeframe}` duration · `{N}` a number · `{topic}` subject · `{method}` the mechanism/system · `{keyword}` comment-trigger word · `{asset}` lead magnet · `{proof}` credibility evidence · `{cta}` the single call to action · `{brand_asset}` distinctive visual/verbal identifier · `{cep}` category entry point

## Data-quality tags used throughout

- **[measured]** — large-sample study with method disclosed (Buffer regression, AuthoredUp medians, IPA databank)
- **[reported]** — vendor/press-published without methodology; treat as directional or upper bound
- **[case]** — single documented case; generalize the mechanism, not the numbers

## The five invariants (if the model learns nothing else)

1. Hook before value; the first 1.5–3 seconds / first line decides distribution.
2. One CTA per asset. Multiple CTAs depress all of them.
3. At zero followers, engagement given > content posted: comments, collabs and search are the only follower-independent distribution channels.
4. Replying to comments lifts engagement on every platform measured. Conversation is the universal ranking currency.
5. Specificity beats adjectives; original beats trend-chasing; consistency (weekly streaks) beats volume bursts.

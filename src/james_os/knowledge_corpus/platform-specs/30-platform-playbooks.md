# Platform Playbooks (2025–26)

Benchmark caveat: sources measure engagement differently (per-follower / per-reach / per-impression); compare within a source only.

## Instagram

- **Formats:** carousels = engagement leader (saves); Reels = reach leader (2.25× single-image reach). Reels 60–90s best views; 15–30s for replay virality. Brand mix ≈ 8 Reels + 5 carousels + 7 images/month.
- **Specs:** feed 1080×1440 (3:4 primary post-grid-update) or 1080×1350 (4:5); carousel ≤20 slides, first slide locks ratio; Reels 1080×1920, safe zone ~200px top / 400px bottom, cover crops 4:5; Stories keep 250px top/bottom clear.
- **Algorithm:** Mosseri's three: watch time, likes-per-reach, **sends-per-reach** (strongest growth signal). Explore: ~5s view threshold, 95%+ completion. Original content prioritized; watermarked content down-ranked. Trial Reels = non-follower testing (1K+ followers).
- **Search:** captions, spoken words and visuals are indexed; alt text matters. Hashtags: 1–5 precise ("they've never been a good way to increase reach" — Mosseri).
- **Times:** Mon 2–4p, Tue 1–7p, Wed 12–9p, Thu 12–2p local.

## TikTok

- **Formats:** highest-ER major platform (view-based ~3.85–4.2%). 3–10 min videos ≈ 2× the views of 6–10s clips; sweet spots 60–180s substantive / 10–15s trend. Photo carousels: +81% engagement vs video at similar reach [one study]; video wins shares.
- **Specs:** 1080×1920 9:16; no fixed safe zone — avoid bottom ~25–30% and right icon rail.
- **Algorithm:** watch time/completion > replays > shares > comments/follows/saves > likes. Every video gets a ~200–500-viewer test batch; ~70% completion promotes to next batch.
- **Search:** 65% of Gen Z use TikTok as a search engine; spoken keywords in first 2–3s weigh most; captions keywords +20–40% visibility; few niche hashtags only.
- **Times:** weekdays 1–6p local.

## YouTube (+ Shorts)

- **Formats:** Shorts bimodal ~13s or ~60s (30–45s underperforms); long-form = revenue ($1.61–29.30 RPM vs $0.01–0.06 Shorts). Shorts for discovery → long-form for depth.
- **Specs:** long-form 16:9 1920×1080+; thumbnail 1280×720 <2MB; Shorts 1080×1920, safe center ~1080×1420 (225px top / 575px bottom).
- **Algorithm:** watch time, CTR, retention curves, satisfaction surveys; Shorts ranked on viewed-vs-swiped, replays, shares. Deliver on the thumbnail promise — clickbait that tanks retention is buried.
- **Benchmarks:** CTR 4–5% average (>10% exceptional); Shorts: ≥70% avg-viewed, >80% 3s retention, 3–6% like:view.
- **Cadence/times:** 1–2 long-form + 3–5 Shorts/week; long-form weekdays 2–4p (Sun 8–10a best); Shorts 12–2p & 6–7p — near-opposite windows.

## LinkedIn

- **Formats:** documents/PDF carousels #1 (~7.0% ER per impressions; Buffer method 21.77% — highest format anywhere); polls over-index reach (1.64×); link posts lose (3.3%). Video = growth format (impressions +73%) but per-post views falling.
- **Personal vs page:** personal 5–8× page engagement; same carousel +63% on personal; employee comments in first hour = 5–10× amplification; comments beat reshares.
- **Specs:** docs ≤300 pages/100MB, best 1080×1350, 5–15 slides; images 1200×627 / 1200×1200 / 720×900; 3,000-char posts.
- **Algorithm:** dwell time + comments > reactions; golden hour (first 30–60 min); external links suppressed; hashtags near-dead (6+ hurts). Max 1 post/24h per profile; 2–5/week.
- **Times:** Tue 11a–5p, Wed 11a–4p, Thu 11a & 1–5p.

## X (Twitter)

- **Formats:** threads ≈ 3× single-post engagement; native video ~10× text engagement in ranking analyses; lowest-ER major platform overall.
- **Algorithm weights (open-source analyses):** reply-with-author-response ~150× a like; quote ~25×; repost ~20×; reply ~13.5–27×; profile click ~12×; bookmark ~10×. Half-life ~6h (post is done at 24h) → 2–5 posts/day. Premium = 2–4× reach. Links −50–90% reach (put in reply). Hashtags obsolete; 3+ = spam signal.
- **Specs:** images 1600×900 / 1080×1080; video ≤2:20/512MB standard, 9:16 goes full-screen mobile; 71–100 chars ideal copy.
- **Times:** Mon 2–3p, Tue–Thu 12–6p; test 8–10a; Wed 9a peak.

## Facebook

- **Formats:** the organic comeback (reach +51%, interactions +56% YoY [Metricool]); format gaps small; links lose (0.05%). All video is Reels since June 2025 (no length caps); short 7–15s wins discovery. ~50% of feed is recommended content.
- **Specs:** feed 1080×1350/1080×1080; Reels/Stories 1080×1920; link image 1200×628; copy 1–80 chars ideal.
- **Algorithm:** dwell 15s+ tracked; 5+ word comments ≈ 3× weight; private Messenger/WhatsApp shares = strong signal; demoted: engagement bait, undisclosed AI content, re-aggregated posts. Groups = deepest interactions (1.8B monthly engagers).
- **Times:** Mon 12–1p, Tue–Wed 12–8p, Thu 12–2p & 8p. Cadence 2–5 feed posts/week + Reels.

## Pinterest

- **Formats:** static 2:3 pins dominate discovery volume (89% of viral pins); video pins win ER; +23% YoY engagement (growing). Evergreen: 60%+ of saves on pins older than 90 days.
- **Specs:** 1000×1500 (2:3); video 6–15s sweet spot; title ≤100 (front-load 40); description 220–232 chars ≤5 keywords.
- **Algorithm/SEO:** a search engine — 96% unbranded queries; keyword stack: title → description → board title/desc → alt text → overlay text; first board saved to sets categorization; fresh pins (new images) beat repins; alt text +25% impressions / +123% outbound clicks; pin seasonal 45–60 days ahead. Links are rewarded here (the outlier).
- **Times:** weekday mornings; Mon 10a–12p, Wed 8a–1p.

# Benchmarks & Targets (2025–26)

**Method warning:** engagement rate has three formulas — by follower, by reach, by impression. A "0.5%" and a "5%" benchmark can describe the same performance. Always compare within one source/method.

## Engagement-rate medians by platform [measured]

| Platform | Median ER | Method | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2.6% (view-based ~3.85–4.2%) | follower / view | Still #1; declining ~10%/yr |
| Instagram | 0.45% | follower | Carousels 0.50 > Reels 0.48 > images 0.33 |
| LinkedIn | 5.2% (docs ~7.0%) | impression | The one platform where ER rose YoY |
| Facebook | 0.15% | impression | Links 0.05% — the loser format |
| X | 0.10% | follower | Most industries "near zero" |
| Pinterest | 0.2–0.5% | follower | Video pins 5.75% by Buffer's method |

- Small accounts run higher: expect 3–5%+ on visual platforms under 5K followers, declining toward medians with scale.
- Persona-driven outliers: Duolingo 3.4% IG (~7× median); Semrush 7.16% B2B social (~10× norm).

## Follower-growth benchmarks

- Healthy organic early-stage: **5–10%/month** (≈50–100/month under 1K).
- Comment-loop alone: ~100/month (Instagram $1.80) [reported]; systematic LinkedIn commenting: 200–500/month [reported].
- Engagement %s and growth %s both decline with scale — benchmark against stage, not aspiration.

## Time-to-milestone (organic, consistent execution)

| Milestone | Typical | Fast documented | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1K | YouTube avg 254 days; IG/X ~2–4 months with daily engagement; TikTok days-to-weeks | YouTube 123 days SEO-led; TikTok single-video | High variance |
| 10K | **6–12 months** systematic execution | 30-day TikTok runs documented | TikTok fastest; YouTube/X slowest (12–24 mo) |
| 100K | **2–4 years** compounding | Duolingo ~5 months (brand outlier) | Requires systemization stage |

## Posting-frequency targets [measured, Buffer 2026]

| Platform | Minimum | Better |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 2–5/week | 6–10+/week |
| Instagram | 3–5/week (mix: ~8 Reels + 5 carousels + 7 images/mo for brands) | 6–9/week |
| LinkedIn | 2–5/week, max 1/day per profile | — |
| X | 3–5/day | up to 10 |
| Facebook | 2–5/week + Reels | 1–2/day |
| YouTube | 1–2 long-form/week | + 3–5 Shorts/week |
| Pinterest | 3–5 fresh pins/week new accounts | 1–3/day established |

- Consistency rule: 20+ active weeks per 26 ≈ 5× per-post engagement vs sporadic [measured]. Never gap 2+ weeks.

## Best posting windows (Sprout, ~2B engagements — local time)

- Consensus: **weekday early-to-mid afternoon**; weekends underperform for brands.
- Instagram: Mon 2–4p, Tue 1–7p, Wed 12–9p. TikTok: weekdays 1–6p. LinkedIn: Tue–Thu 11a–5p. X: Mon–Thu 12–6p (test 8–10a). Facebook: Tue–Wed 12–8p. Pinterest: mornings (the exception). YouTube long-form: weekdays 2–4p (Sunday 8–10a best single slot); Shorts 12–2p & 6–7p.

## Health metrics beyond followers

- **YouTube CTR:** 4–5% average; 4–7% good; >10% exceptional. Shorts: ≥70% average-viewed, >80% first-3s retention, 3–6% like:view.
- **Instagram Reels:** watch time, likes-per-reach, sends-per-reach (Mosseri's three).
- **Carousel health:** 60–75% slide-1→2 swipe-through; 25–40% completion; 1.5–3% save rate = strong.
- **Response ops:** reply SLA <24h (73% defect on silence); first-hour replies on own posts.
- **Stage KPIs:** 0→1K: comments given/day, classifier consistency. 1K→10K: format retention baselines, email capture rate. 10K→100K: repurposing throughput, community activity. 100K+: share of search, owned-channel size, pricing power.

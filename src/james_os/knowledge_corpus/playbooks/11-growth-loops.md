# Growth Loops (Executable Form)

## Loop 1 — Comment-to-Follower

**Procedure:** SELECT 10–30 target posts/day (larger accounts in niche, posted <4h ago) → POST a value-adding comment (15+ words, specific, no links, no generic praise) → comment earns impressions from the host audience → profile visits → follows → new followers' engagement improves your own posts' distribution.

**Variants:**
- *The $1.80 method (Instagram/hashtags):* 10 niche hashtags × top 9 posts each = 90 genuine "two cents" comments/day. Expected: ~100 followers/month early, compounding. [reported]
- *Tiered LinkedIn system:* Tier 1 daily (authors with 50K–500K-impression posts), Tier 2 3–4×/week (responsive communities), Tier 3 1–2×/week (niche authorities). 15 comments/day ≈ 30 min. Throughput: one good comment on a 50K-impression post ≈ 200–500 profile visits; ≈50–125 followers/week at ~5% visit→follow. [reported, n=1 tracked]

**Rules:** within 4 hours of the post; ≤5 sentences; add information, ask a smart question, or give a specific compliment; never pitch.

## Loop 2 — Collab / Duet

**Procedure:** IDENTIFY peers at 0.5–2× your size → PROPOSE collab (TikTok duet/stitch, IG Collab post = one post in both feeds, LinkedIn co-post, X space, YouTube guest) → mutual audience exposure → follower exchange → larger size unlocks bigger partners → repeat upward.
**Brand variant:** product seeding — free product is collab currency at zero followers (Gymshark case).

## Loop 3 — Search-Content (SEO)

**Procedure:** RESEARCH queries in niche (YouTube search, TikTok search, Pinterest, Google autocomplete) → PUBLISH content titled to the query → ranks and accrues views independent of follower count → subscribers arrive pre-qualified → their watch behavior reveals new queries → repeat.
**Evidence:** SEO-titled YouTube channel reached 1K subs in 123 days vs the 254-day average. [case]

## Loop 4 — Repurposing Flywheel (the Pyramid)

**Procedure:** PRODUCE one pillar asset/week (podcast, keynote, long video, deep-dive article) → ATOMIZE into 10–30 platform-native pieces (clips with new hooks, carousels, threads, quote graphics, newsletter section) → each piece points attention back to the pillar → engagement data on pieces determines the next pillar topic.
**Documented scales:** 1 keynote → 30+ pieces → 35M views (GaryVee); 250+ pieces/week via 3-level format transformation: L1 same asset cross-posted, L2 long-form → thread → posts, L3 threads → carousels/slide decks (Hormozi). [case]

## Loop 5 — Community Flywheel

**Procedure:** CONTENT attracts members → community produces UGC/templates/answers → brand FORMALIZES the best (official gallery, ambassador title, repost, feature) → featured members create more and recruit peers → community output becomes onboarding content for newcomers.
**Cases:** Notion (user template gallery, waitlisted ambassador program, 50+ language communities), Glossier (UGC as primary content), Gymshark (#Gymshark66 challenge + athletes). Rule: institutionalize what fans already do; don't invent behavior.

## Loop 6 — Email ↔ Social

**Procedure:** social post → comment-keyword CTA → DM automation delivers {asset} → newsletter capture → newsletter deep-dives become next week's social posts → subscribers reshare → new social reach → more capture.
**Note:** email is the de-risking asset — the only audience the algorithm can't take away.

## Loop 7 — Comment-to-DM (automatable)

**Procedure:** post with "comment {keyword}" CTA → automation DMs the {asset} to each commenter → comment volume spikes → algorithm expands post reach → more commenters → DM list becomes an owned channel.
**Benchmarks [reported, vendor — treat as ceilings]:** DM automation opens 80–100% (email 17–35%), replies 50–60%, CTR up to 28%; documented case: +901% follower growth in 12 days during a comment-to-DM campaign; conversion 5–15% vs 1–3% for link-in-bio.

## Loop selection by stage

| Stage | Primary loops |
|---|---|
| 0→1K | 1 (comment), 3 (search), 2 (peer collabs) |
| 1K→10K | 1 + 3 continue, 7 (comment-to-DM), 2 (bigger collabs) |
| 10K→100K | 4 (repurposing), 6 (email), 5 (community), 2 |
| 100K+ | 4 + 5 + 6 at scale; earned-media flywheel |

# Case Study Library

Format per case: **Mechanism → Numbers → Rule.** All numbers are [case]/[reported] — company/press-sourced, not audited.

## Brand accounts

### Duolingo (TikTok)
**Mechanism:** persistent "unhinged" mascot character (Duo) with continuity across videos; trend-hijacking within hours; comment section treated as a content surface (in-character replies on its own and others' videos); self-aware about its guilt-trip-notification reputation.
**Numbers:** ~50K → 10M+ TikTok followers ("40× in under 5 months" during breakout); "Death of Duo" campaign: 25,000% mention increase, 90M+ views, DAU +62%; one comment-reply earned 1.27M engagements; 80% of growth attributed organic; 3.4% Instagram ER (~7× median).
**Rule:** a recurring character + fast trend response + comments-as-content converts a brand account into a serialized entertainment channel; the product is the punchline, not the pitch.

### Ryanair (TikTok)
**Mechanism:** self-deprecation as trust — say the things customers mock you for before they do; personified aircraft filter; heavy comment participation.
**Numbers:** 2M+ followers, 21M+ likes since May 2020; #Ryanair 2.6B+ views; famously tiny team.
**Rule:** own your flaws publicly before the audience weaponizes them; self-deprecation is the cheapest authenticity signal a brand can buy.

### Wendy's (X)
**Mechanism:** invited users to request roasts (audience becomes the content engine); playful public fights with larger competitors for shareable conflict.
**Numbers:** roast tweets routinely earned tens of thousands of engagements; produced the most-retweeted tweet of its era (#NuggsForCarter); documented store-traffic lift.
**Rule:** a sharp consistent persona + user-invited interaction turns replies into the highest-reach format; playful conflict is distribution.

### Gymshark (ambassador model)
**Mechanism:** founder mailed free apparel to the fitness YouTubers he personally watched (pre-"influencer marketing"); converted the best into named "Gymshark Athletes" (identity, not sponsorship), selected on values/engagement over follower count; bridged online→offline with expos where fans meet athletes.
**Numbers:** $1.45B valuation; 80–100+ athletes; #gymshark 11.5B TikTok views; 6–8-hour pop-up queues; #Gymshark66 UGC challenge engine.
**Rule:** seed product to micro/mid creators whose audience trust exceeds their price tag; convert transactions into identity; let creators be the brand's face.

### Glossier (community-led)
**Mechanism:** built the Into The Gloss blog audience FIRST (interview format = borrowed authority), then launched products the community asked for; customer UGC as primary content; top customers as reps.
**Numbers:** ~1.5M monthly blog readers pre-launch; $1.2–1.8B valuation with the large majority of growth from peer/owned channels.
**Rule:** audience before product — build a trust asset in the niche, then co-design and co-market with the community.

### HubSpot (LinkedIn meme turn)
**Mechanism:** pivoted from polished corporate posts to memes, "shower musings," iPhone-quality video in practitioner voice; every post filtered through: Who is this for? What's our angle? How does it connect to culture?
**Numbers:** +84% LinkedIn growth in six months; shares up.
**Rule:** in B2B, the practitioner's daily pain is the meme; relatability outperforms authority-posturing ~10× on engagement.

### Semrush (recurring meme franchises)
**Mechanism:** recurring microcontent franchises (#StruggleSearch) built on marketer in-jokes.
**Numbers:** 250K+ impressions, 17.9K engagements, 7.16% average ER in month one (B2B norm <1%).
**Rule:** recurring franchises beat one-off jokes — own a repeatable format.

### Notion (community-led growth)
**Mechanism:** users spontaneously built templates/communities; Notion formalized rather than controlled — official gallery from user templates, waitlisted ambassador program requiring demonstrated leadership, communities in 50+ languages.
**Rule:** watch what fans already do, then institutionalize it; UGC that helps new users onboard is a growth loop, not just content.

### Liquid Death
**Mechanism:** "a content studio disguised as a beverage company" — entertainment-first, zero product-benefit content; converted hate comments into an album (2M+ organic views); scarcity collabs (100-unit Tony Hawk blood boards → 800% search spike); merch + 225K-member loyalty club.
**Numbers:** $1.4B valuation; ~14M followers; 40%+ of Gen Z adults have tried it.
**Rule:** "entertainment is the new awareness" — make content people would watch if the product didn't exist; weaponize detractors; satire + sincerity = shareability.

## Founder/creator systems

### Gary Vaynerchuk — content pyramid + $1.80
**Mechanism (pyramid):** one daily pillar (keynote/podcast/vlog) → team atomizes into micro-content contextualized per platform → micro drives attention back to pillar → comment data informs next pillar. 1 keynote → 30+ pieces → 35M+ views.
**Mechanism ($1.80):** 10 niche hashtags × top 9 posts = 90 genuine comments/day ("two cents" × 90). Rules: real value only, ~2 min/post, no pitching. ~100 followers/month early, compounding.
**Rule:** distribution is a system, not an act; attention is bought with genuine engagement before content.

### Alex Hormozi — volume via format transformation
**Mechanism:** 250+ pieces/week from a 3-level repurposing stack: L1 same asset cross-posted; L2 long-form → thread → LinkedIn posts; L3 threads → carousels/TikTok slide decks. Create once, transform formats; documented content team (~$70K+/month at peak), now AI-assisted.
**Rule:** volume comes from transformation, not ideation; one idea should exist in every format.

### Justin Welsh — solo LinkedIn system
**Mechanism:** daily post at fixed time; comment-first warmup on target accounts before posting; plain-text formatting; one niche (solopreneurship); newsletter CTA in profile, not posts.
**Numbers:** ~500K+ LinkedIn followers; ~$5M+/yr solo business.
**Rule:** a solo operator can compound with cadence + niche discipline + owned-channel capture; sell in the profile, give in the feed.

### Sahil Bloom — pillar threads + deliberate broadening
**Mechanism:** 2×/week pillar threads as "blog posts"; manual distribution at zero (posted first thread as replies under ~50 relevant accounts until a 300K-follower RT broke it out); internal-linking loops (roundup threads, thread-of-threads archive); data-driven topic testing before big pieces; broadened beyond finance at ~100K after observing finance-only accounts plateau.
**Numbers:** ~500 → 187K followers in year one; 937K in 3 years; newsletter 400K+ subs in 36 months; peer "100K Club" group chat for systematic cross-promotion.
**Rule:** pillar content + manual seeding under relevant accounts is the zero-follower breakout path; niche until ~100K, then broaden; peer networks compound.

## Meta-patterns across all cases

1. **Persona/voice is the moat** — every breakout brand account has a describable character.
2. **Comments are a content surface**, not support tickets (Duolingo, Ryanair, Wendy's).
3. **Other people's audiences first** (Gymshark seeding, Bloom's reply-seeding, Glossier's interviews).
4. **Formalize community behavior** rather than inventing it (Notion, Gymshark, Glossier).
5. **Recurring franchises** beat one-offs (Semrush, Duolingo's serialized mascot).
6. **Entertainment-first at brand level; specificity-first at founder level.**

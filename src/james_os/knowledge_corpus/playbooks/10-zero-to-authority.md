# Zero-to-Authority: The Stage-Gated Model

Growth is not linear — tactics that work at 0 followers stop working at 10K, and vice versa. The controlling variables at every stage are **consistency and engagement**, not virality [measured: Buffer ~2M posts].

## Stage 0 → 1K — "Borrowed Audiences" (months 1–3, up to 6)

**Why posting into the void fails:** at 0 followers, owned distribution ≈ 0 on follower-graph platforms. Only three distribution channels exist:
1. **Algorithmic test audiences** — TikTok shows every video to ~200–500 users regardless of followers.
2. **Other people's audiences** — comments, collabs, guest content.
3. **Search** — YouTube/Pinterest/Google/TikTok-search content surfaces by query, not graph.

**Do:**
- Finalize positioning: niche, bio one-liner (templates/26), pinned proof post. One niche only.
- 4–7 posts/week on 2–3 platforms max; 10–15 niche-consistent posts to train platform classifiers.
- **30+ min/day of engagement given:** strategic commenting (the $1.80 method / tiered commenting — playbooks/11 loop #1).
- Search-optimized formats: title to queries, keywords in captions/voiceover.
- Collabs with peers at your size; seed product/value to micro-creators if a brand.

**Don't:** chase trends, post links, broadcast without engaging, split across 5 platforms.

**Benchmarks:** 5–10% monthly follower growth (≈50–100/month at this stage); engagement given should exceed content posted.

**Exit criteria (→ Stage 2):** 1K followers OR a format with repeatable above-baseline retention; platform unlocks arrive here (Instagram Trial Reels at 1K, YouTube monetization at 1K, TikTok LIVE at 1K).

## Stage 1K → 10K — "Format–Market Fit" (months 3–12)

**Do:**
- Double down on the 1–2 formats analytics show working; kill the rest.
- 80/20 value-to-promo ratio; first-hour reply discipline (reply within 5–10 min of posting).
- Peer collabs with accounts 0.5–2× your size; begin recurring franchises (a named series).
- Start email capture (comment-keyword CTAs → DM → list).
- Run every experimental concept as an Instagram Trial Reel / small-batch test first.

**Benchmarks:** engagement rates run higher at small size — expect 3–5%+ on visual platforms under 5K, declining toward platform medians as you scale.

**Exit criteria:** 10K followers OR consistent inbound (DMs, mentions, collab requests) without outbound effort.

## Stage 10K → 100K — "Systemization" (year 1–3)

**Do:**
- Replace ad-hoc creation with a repurposing system: one pillar asset/week → 10–30 platform-native pieces (playbooks/11 loop #4).
- Launch community program (Discord/Skool/Circle) and/or formalize UGC (galleries, features, ambassador titles).
- Cross-platform expansion; newsletter becomes a first-class channel.
- Build peer network at your tier for systematic cross-promotion (documented "100K Club" pattern).
- **Broaden the niche to adjacent topics** — this is a 100K-stage move, not a 0-follower move (niche-only accounts plateau near 100K).

**Exit criteria:** 100K OR owned channels (email/community) large enough to de-risk algorithm dependence.

## Stage 100K+ — "Media Company"

- Owned channels (newsletter, community, podcast) de-risk algorithms; monetization diversifies.
- Team/AI leverage replaces founder hours; founder voice stays on pillar content, team atomizes.
- Brand-level moves unlock: earned media flywheels, big collabs, category campaigns (see 12-case-studies: Duolingo at this stage attributed 80% of growth to organic/word-of-mouth).

## Stage-invariant rules

1. Consistency floor: never miss 2 consecutive weeks; 20+ active weeks per 26 ≈ 5× per-post engagement.
2. Reply to every comment while volume allows; protect the first hour.
3. Engagement given scales down as audience scales up — but never to zero.
4. Each stage's tactic becomes the next stage's system: manual comments → community management; manual repurposing → pipeline; personal collabs → ambassador program.

# Cold-Start Tactics Per Platform (2025–26)

**Universal doctrine:** at zero followers, **engagement given > content posted.** Comments, collabs and search are the only channels where distribution doesn't depend on an audience you don't have.

## TikTok
- **Mechanism:** every upload gets a ~200–500-viewer test batch via the interest graph; follower count irrelevant to initial reach. ~70% completion needed for second-batch promotion.
- **Play:** post 10–15 niche-consistent videos to train the classifier; optimize completion (hook <1.5s, length where completion holds); search-keyword captions and spoken keywords; duet/stitch larger creators.
- **Expectation:** fastest, highest-variance platform — a single test-batch winner can reach 1K in days.

## Instagram
- **Mechanism:** Trial Reels (requires 1K followers) distribute only to non-followers in waves over 72h; auto-promote if beating niche-relative baselines (watch time, replays, shares, like/save-to-view).
- **Play under 1K:** Reels + SEO captions + $1.80-style niche commenting; collab posts (one post, two feeds) with peers.
- **Play at 1K+:** run every experimental concept as a Trial Reel first; double down on winners.

## LinkedIn
- **Mechanism:** comments surface to the commenter's network AND the post's audience; feed rewards dwell + early comments; personal profiles get 5–8× page engagement.
- **Play:** 15 comments/day tiered system (Tier 1 daily: 50K–500K-impression authors; Tier 2: responsive communities; Tier 3: niche authorities) for 2–4 weeks BEFORE/alongside posting — build the warm audience first. Then 2–5 posts/week, max 1/day.
- **Throughput [reported]:** 200–500 profile visits per good comment on a big post; 50–125 followers/week from ~30 min/day.

## YouTube
- **Mechanism:** search + suggested are follower-independent; subscribers arrive pre-qualified.
- **Play:** search-first titles targeting long-tail queries; consistent format/franchise; Shorts for discovery (expect low sub-conversion); thumbnails as the #1 CTR lever.
- **Expectation:** slowest compounding: average 254 days to 1K subs; 31% of channels needed 150+ uploads; SEO-led channels documented at 123 days.

## X (Twitter)
- **Mechanism:** timeline reach is graph-dependent → replies under large accounts are the only free distribution at zero. Time decay: posts lose ~half their visibility score every 6 hours.
- **Play:** 10–30 fast, substantive replies/day under large niche accounts (speed matters — early replies capture the impressions); pillar threads seeded manually as replies under ~50 relevant accounts (the documented Bloom breakout). X Premium = 2–4× reach boost.
- **Cadence:** 2–5 posts/day once established; >10/day dilutes.

## Pinterest
- **Mechanism:** pure search engine — 96% of searches unbranded; followers nearly irrelevant to distribution; evergreen accrual (60%+ of saves land on Pins older than 90 days).
- **Play:** keyword-stack every field (title → description → board → alt text → overlay text); 2:3 vertical with benefit text overlay; multiple fresh Pin designs per content piece; pin seasonally 45–60 days ahead.

## Facebook
- **Mechanism:** ~50% of feed is recommended (unconnected) content — every post competes for discovery; Reels are the non-follower surface.
- **Play:** short Reels (7–15s) for discovery; Groups for depth (own or participate — 1.8B monthly Group engagers); short copy (1–80 chars).

## Platform priority heuristic for a new brand

1. Pick ONE short-video discovery platform (TikTok or IG Reels) + ONE search platform (YouTube or Pinterest) + the niche's conversation platform (LinkedIn for B2B, X for tech/finance, Facebook Groups for local).
2. Founder-led B2B: LinkedIn (comments-first) + one video platform.
3. Visual/commerce product: Instagram + TikTok + Pinterest.
4. Local/community business: Facebook (page + Groups) + Instagram + Google (search/maps content).
